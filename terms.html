Platform use

Demo terms and limitations.

These terms explain the intended use of the local MACTORY FATCH concept. They are not production marketplace terms prepared for a named legal operator.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

ScopeDemo dataAI researchDue diligencePermitted useBefore launch
Prototype only. The content below is for a college/demo prototype and is not legal advice or a complete enforceable contract. Production use requires review by a qualified lawyer for the operator and markets served.

1. Scope

MACTORY FATCH is a prototype interface intended to help users organize manufacturing requirements, prepare sourcing requests. It does not currently represent a fully operating marketplace or procurement intermediary.

2. Demo data and outputs

There are currently no manufacturer profiles in the directory. MACTORY FATCH does not claim any factory has registered. Any future supplier profile must be grounded in evidence and reviewed before publication. Do not make purchasing, investment, safety or regulatory decisions based on demo records.

3. AI and research tools

AI-assisted brief analysis and supplier research, if configured, can produce errors, incomplete summaries, stale results and incorrect matches. Web search links indicate sources that may be relevant but are not proof of authenticity. The app must not imply that a source citation equals an independent verification. AI tools do not replace professional, technical, financial, legal or quality-control advice.

4. Independent due diligence

Before transacting with any manufacturer, independently confirm legal identity and status, authority of the contact, the physical production site, manufacturing capacity, quality systems, certificates with the issuing body, samples, references, commercial terms, payment instructions, intellectual property terms, product compliance and inspection arrangements. Registration alone is not proof of factory capability or good performance.

5. Permitted use

Do not use the prototype to submit false registration details, impersonate a business, upload information you are not authorized to share, probe the server for secrets, bypass access controls, or send confidential material to optional AI functions. Do not treat local RFQs as delivered or acknowledged by suppliers. The current app stores demo entries in browser storage and does not dispatch them to real factories.

6. Production launch requirements

Before commercial launch, the operator must publish complete marketplace terms that identify the legal entity and address, define eligibility, listing and verification rules, fees if any, communication and transaction responsibilities, content ownership and licenses, moderation, dispute handling, liability allocation, governing law and other provisions appropriate to the actual service. Those terms must be reviewed for the jurisdictions in which the service operates.
