About MACTORY FATCH

A clearer starting point for manufacturing.

Manufacturing projects often start with incomplete specifications and a scattered search for capable suppliers. MACTORY FATCH is a concept for making that first step more structured.

Structure the brief

Help buyers describe materials, quantities, location, processes, standards and unanswered questions before reaching out.

Explain matches

When genuine manufacturers join, show the criteria behind a match and separate stated capability from independently verified evidence.

Respect evidence

Keep supplier statements, AI-generated leads, source-linked claims and independently verified facts clearly separate.

The physical world behind the product

Manufacturing is more than a search result.

Equipment, materials, people, tolerances and quality systems all matter when choosing who should make a product.

Illustrative generated industrial imagery, not customer or supplier testimonials.

Product status

Prototype today. A real marketplace takes more.

The current product is a prototype with an empty registered manufacturer directory, browser-based drafts and optional server-side AI. It does not have production buyer accounts, supplier accounts, live RFQ delivery, automated government-record verification, or a production database. These limitations must be resolved before using it for real procurement.
