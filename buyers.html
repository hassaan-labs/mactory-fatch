Search tips

A better brief produces better research.

  • Include the finished product and how it will be used.
  • Specify material, grade, dimensions and process where known.
  • State order quantity and whether repeat orders are likely.
  • Add delivery state, target date, standards and testing needs.
  • Mark unknowns rather than inventing values.
Verification matters. Source links show where a statement appeared—not that the statement is true. Confirm legal details, factory location, capability and certification independently.
